## 4. Word bank vetting

Read all 1,553 words of the original `src/activities/word-search/words/words.json`. Policy applied:

- US spellings dropped (UK/Australian kept where present): CENTER, COLOR, FAVOR, FAVORITE, FLAVOR, GRAY, HARBOR, HUMOR,
  JEWELRY, NEIGHBOR, NEIGHBORHOOD, THEATER, APOLOGIZE, TIRE. FAVOUR, FAVOURITE, GREY and THEATRE remain. The
  UK forms of the others (CENTRE, COLOUR, ...) are not in the raw list, so those words are simply gone.
- Also dropped: US-only vocabulary, months/days (proper nouns), titles/names, religious/occult words, alcohol and meat
  words (food sensitivities; judgment call, easy to revert), medical, sad/morbid/violent/negative words, words
  disrespectful to an elderly solver (ELDER, ELDERLY, ANCIENT), inflected forms whose base is in the bank (WOMEN,
  CHILDREN, TEETH, ...), trivial plurals/verb forms, function-like words (JUST, SOME, STILL, ...) and odd jargon.
- 3-letter blocklist entries inside innocent words (CLASS, BASS, TITLE, HELLO, NIGHT) were left alone per the agreed design.
- These exclusions took the bank to 1,393 (< 1,500). `CUTOFF` was raised 7500 -> 7800 -> 8000 -> 8300. Each time the
  newly added words were read; most were first names, places, brands, abbreviations and bureaucratic jargon and were
  excluded, and the useful ones were kept (130 new words kept, e.g. BUTTERFLY, SPICE, PASTA, CURRY, FOUNTAIN, TURTLE).

Counts: `data/exclude.txt` grew by 525 entries (160 original-bank words + 365 words pulled in by the raised cutoff and
rejected). Final bank: **1,523 words** (>= 1,500; a 50-puzzle batch needs 600). `pnpm build:words` re-run twice,
byte-identical output (deterministic). The margin over 1,500 is thin (23): further exclusions will need another small `CUTOFF` bump.
